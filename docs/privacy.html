Privacy Policy — Blue Crane Browser

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Blue Crane Browser ("the app", "we", "us") is a lightweight Windows web browser. This policy explains what data the app collects, stores, and shares.

Summary

Blue Crane Browser does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no user accounts.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app writes stays on your device, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BlueCrane:

  • Settings (settings.json) — your chosen search engine and whether first-time setup has been completed.
  • Error log (error.log) — technical details of any crash, written locally so you can review or share them if you report a bug. This log is never uploaded automatically.

Suspended and open tabs' page state is held in memory or in the underlying browser engine's local cache for the current session; it is not sent anywhere by the app.

None of this data is transmitted to us or to any third party. We do not operate any server that the app talks to.

Browsing activity

When you navigate to a website or run a search, Blue Crane Browser uses the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime to load and render pages. That traffic goes directly between your device and the website or search engine you're visiting — it does not pass through us. That activity is subject to:

  • the privacy policy of the website or search engine you visit (including the search engine you've selected in settings: WebCrawler, Google, Bing, or DuckDuckGo), and
  • Microsoft's own privacy statement for the WebView2/Edge component, available at privacy.microsoft.com.

Children's privacy

Blue Crane Browser is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a revised effective date.
